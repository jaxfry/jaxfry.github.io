**Hello Everyone!** This year was my third time at Anime Revolution, and I had such a blast. It was awesome seeing all the cosplaying. Some of them were so meticulous and insane. So cool. I ended up getting some awesome posters, stickers, and prints (more about that later), and overall it was just such a fun time. I'm already excited to hopefully go again next year!

## Panels

I always love going to the screenings, and this year there were three featured anime. The big one was *Black Torch*. I'd never heard of it either, and I actually missed the screening because it was early in the morning and I didn't want to get out of bed. I missed the discussion panel as well, so I still don't know anything about it. But hey, that's okay.

There were also screenings of *Rich Girl Caretaker* and *Mebius Dust*, which were interesting to experience for the first time at the convention, not necessarily something I typically watch, but it was really cool hearing the sound director (Takashi Takadera)'s notes. It's awesome to see him every year and just seeing how passionate he is about his work. Sadly, I didn't get a photograph with him like I did last year :(

Oh yeah, another panel they do which is so awesome is the live recording. The voice actors from *Rich Girl Caretaker* did a live recording on stage, and no matter the anime, it's pretty cool seeing them voice act live, with the sound director giving feedback and making slight adjustments. It's subtle, but it is quite noticeable, and I find it interesting every year.

The cultural exchange panel was the one from Saori Araki. It was sort of fun hearing someone's opinions about different Canadian food (their favorite ended up being Timbits as a snack), and then we did a quiz about Canada while the audience did a quiz about Japan.

And I went to a panel about traveling to Japan. It had some great info, and was overall fun.

## Artist Alley

Even the stuff I don't buy is really cool. A whole massive floor of the Vancouver Convention Center, filled to the brim with stalls of artwork. You can sort of walk through it like an art gallery, admiring the artwork and buying the ones you like for a reasonable price... or an unreasonable price, and then not buying that because I would have gone way over budget!

## Fun Extras

They had these really cool cathode ray tube televisions set up at the convention, just for some reason. It turns out that CRT displays look so cool, and I feel like I want my own now... But oh well.

![CRT televisions at the convention]({{ '/assets/images/posts/2026-08-04/crt.jpg' | relative_url }})

While I was walking up and down the artist alley and the vendor booths, I heard a bunch of cheering near one of the stages. I walked over, and there was this random guy doing karaoke, someone you would not expect to be a good singer. He was insane! He was super good, and I stopped and listened, and it was quite interesting just hearing how good of a singer this random guy was.

A random guy also asked me to watch his suitcase while he went to the washroom. It was covered in anime stickers and had two cameras on it. Two! Those lenses are worth so much money. It was cool seeing all the anime stickers on his suitcase, either way.
